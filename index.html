Meet Your Worry Unicorn

Sometimes our body makes a worried feeling. Let's give that feeling a name — and make it a magical unicorn friend!

🧠 First, let's understand feelings

Everyone has a part of their brain called the Worry Brain. Its job is to keep us safe. But sometimes it works TOO hard — like an alarm that goes off when there's no fire!

When your Worry Brain is too loud, you might feel:

😣 tummy hurts 😶 can't find words 🥶 frozen feeling ❓ lots of questions 😮‍💨 hard to breathe

That's NOT you being bad. That's just your Worry Brain being a little too loud. We can help turn down the volume!

🦄 Build Your Worry Unicorn

Your Worry Unicorn lives in your tummy. It's not mean — it's just scared sometimes. Let's get to know it!

When your Worry Unicorn shows up, you can talk to it! Try saying:

"Hi Worry Unicorn. I see you. I know you're trying to help. But I've got this. Let's be brave together." 🦄✨

🌬️ Rainbow Breathing

When your meter is at 3 or higher, try Rainbow Breathing! It sends a message to your Worry Unicorn that you are safe.

🔴 Breathe IN for 4 counts (imagine drawing red)

My Brave Toolkit

Every brave girl needs her tools! These are YOUR special moves for when your Worry Unicorn gets loud.

🛠️ Your Power Tools

🧸
Comfort Anchor
Hold something special — your stuffed animal, a smooth rock, a bracelet. Your hands need something to hold when your belly gets scared.
🌈
Rainbow Breathing
In for 4, hold for 2, out for 4. Imagine painting a rainbow. This sends a calm message to your body.
🔍
5-4-3-2-1 Finder
5 things you SEE · 4 you TOUCH · 3 you HEAR · 2 you SMELL · 1 you TASTE. This wakes up your calm brain!
🎵
Taylor Swift Song
Pick one song that makes you feel powerful. Hum it in your head or listen with headphones. Music is like armor!
🌟
My Special Job
When you have a JOB to do, your brain focuses on helping instead of worrying. Ask Mom or Dad: "What's my job today?"
🤫
Secret Signal
Make a secret signal with Mom or Dad that means "my Worry Unicorn is here." No words needed — just a squeeze of the hand.
🦄
Unicorn Talk
Whisper to your Worry Unicorn: "I see you. We've got this." Talking TO your worry makes it feel smaller.
🏆
Remember a Win
Think of one time you were scared and did it anyway — like the butterflies in Boston! You are braver than you think!

🏃 Move It Out!

Sometimes your body needs to shake the worry out! When your belly meter is high, try one of these moves. Your body and your brain are connected — move your body and your brain starts to calm down too.

🦄
Unicorn Gallop
Gallop around the room like a unicorn for 30 seconds! High knees, arms up!
🫨
Shake It Out
Shake your hands, then arms, then whole body. Shake the worry right off!
🦶
Stomp Breathing
Stomp your feet slowly. Breathe IN on one stomp, OUT on the next. Do 10 stomps.
⭐
Star Jumps
Do 10 jumping jacks! Count out loud. Counting keeps your brain busy while your body moves.
🤗
Bear Hug
Squeeze yourself in a big hug. Squeeze tight, hold for 5 counts, then let go slowly.
🐘
Heavy Feet Walk
Walk super slowly and heavy — like an elephant. Feel the ground under you. You are safe!

💡 Why moving helps

When you're worried, your body gets ready to run or hide. Moving helps your body use up that energy and tells your brain: "We're okay. It's safe now." Tap any move to try it!

💭 The Worry Questions Game

Three questions to ask:

1. "What's the WORST that could happen?"
(Say it out loud — it makes it smaller!)

2. "What would we DO if that happened?"
(You always have a plan!)

3. "What do I think will ACTUALLY happen?"
(Usually... pretty good things!)
